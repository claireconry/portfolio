About me

Hi, I'm Claire.

I help small and growing businesses get real value from the software they choose, and I build the automations that make that experience feel effortless.

My story

The best customer experiences feel personal, and the best systems quietly make that possible at scale.

I started my career in sales development at Scanbot SDK in Bonn, running technical discovery calls with engineering teams. I learned early that the real work starts after the deal: helping people actually succeed with what they've bought.

That led me to customer success. First at TimeKeeper, where I managed 600+ SMB accounts and helped turn trials into long-term customers. Now at OnePageCRM, where I guide customers from first engagement through to confident adoption.

Along the way I got hooked on automation. Every manual follow-up and copy-and-paste task is a chance to build something that responds faster and never forgets.
