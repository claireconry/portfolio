Customer success

Helping customers reach real value.

A customer's first few weeks decide whether they stay. I focus on making that time clear, personal and worth it.

Why it matters to me

Customers don't buy software. They buy the outcome they hope it'll give them.

What I love about customer success is that it sits right where a customer's goals meet the product. My job is to close the gap between "we signed up" and "this has changed how we work".

That means tailoring every demo to the business in front of me, spotting accounts that are drifting before they churn, and creating resources that let customers help themselves. It also means taking what I hear back to the product team.

How I approach it

Three things I focus on for every customer.

Conversion & onboarding

Getting customers to their first win quickly, so the decision to pay is an easy one.

  • Built admin-focused onboarding materials (demo videos, admin guides, setup checklists) that accelerated trial-to-paid conversion
  • Ran product demos that turned 8 prospects into paid customers, worth £11,496 ARR
  • Led consultative calls with trial users, tailoring the product to each business's needs

Engagement & retention

Staying close to customers after they sign up, so they keep getting more from the product.

  • Managed 600+ SMB accounts through proactive engagement and strategic account planning
  • Regular check-ins to track adoption, find upselling opportunities and drive feature adoption
  • Consistent 4–5 star satisfaction ratings, with testimonials praising responsiveness and strategic guidance

Education & activation

Giving customers the resources to help themselves, in language that makes sense to them.

  • Managed a knowledge base of 50+ articles, videos and guides, shaped by support trends and adoption barriers
  • Authored 23+ weekly blog posts and customer case studies
  • Developed industry-specific resources: compliance forms, templates and checklists
  • Partnered with engineering to turn technical functionality into customer-facing resources
