Automations

Building systems that never forget.

Working with hundreds of SMB customers showed me how much time small teams lose to manual follow-up. So I started building the fix.

Why I build them

Every manual task is a chance for a customer to slip through the cracks.

In customer success I see it all the time: a lead that waited too long for a reply, a new customer who got a generic welcome, details retyped from an inbox at the end of the day.

Automation is how I solve those problems for good. I connect the tools businesses already use, such as CRMs, forms, payments and email, so the right thing happens the moment something changes. At OnePageCRM I've also worked with an external development partner to define a Luma integration, packaged as a scalable solution for businesses with that use case.

What they fix

Small teams lose hours to work a system could do in seconds.

  • Leads going cold
    Every enquiry answered

    Someone fills in a form and hears back straight away. The owner is notified, the follow-up is scheduled, and nobody slips through.

  • One-size welcomes
    The right message for each customer

    New customers are routed by what they bought, so premium members feel premium, and everyone else gets a gentle nudge to upgrade.

  • Copy, paste, repeat
    Records that keep themselves

    Details land in the CRM or spreadsheet automatically, tagged and organised, instead of being retyped by hand.
